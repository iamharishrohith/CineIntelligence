## 🌟 What Makes CineIntelligence™ Unique?

### 1. Pan-India Star Synergy Engine
Traditional film box-office predictors only evaluate single static variables (e.g. genre or budget). **CineIntelligence™** introduces a dynamic **Star Synergy Engine** that calculates interactive reputation scores across:
- Director Reputation Index ($1.0 - 10.0$)
- Lead Actor & Actress Star Power
- Music Director Track Record
- Studio Banner Commercial Acclaim
- Multiplier Product: $\text{Synergy} = \text{Director Index} \times \text{Cast Index}$

### 2. Multi-Currency Global Budget Normalization
Film budgets across global markets are submitted in different currencies and denominations. CineIntelligence™ features a real-time currency conversion engine supporting:
- **Currencies**: `INR (₹)`, `USD ($)`, `EUR (€)`, `GBP (£)`
- **Denominations**: `Crores`, `Lakhs`, `Millions`, `Thousands`
- **Output**: Automatically converts and normalizes all budgets to USD before feeding into the ML feature vector.
