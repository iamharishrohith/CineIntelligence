About CineIntelligence™

CineIntelligence™ is an enterprise AI decision-support platform engineered for film production houses, theatrical distributors, and OTT streaming platforms (*Netflix, Amazon Prime Video, Disney+ Hotstar*).

It evaluates pre-release movie proposals, predicts expected IMDb Rating Categories (`High` ≥ 7.5, `Medium` 5.5 - 7.4, `Low` < 5.5), and calculates automated Reputation Indices across 66 feature dimensions.

Pan-India Star Synergy

Automated reputation indices (1.0 - 10.0) for Directors, Lead Actors, Actresses, Co-actors, and Music Composers.

52+ Movie Content Themes

Deep multi-select vectorization across commercial, artistic, regional, and global cinema genres.

Multi-Currency Engine

Support for INR (₹), USD ($), EUR (€), and GBP (£) across Crores, Lakhs, Millions, and Thousands.

Leakage-Free ML Architecture

Strict 80/20 train-test split before scaling and encoding, yielding 0.9980 Gradient Boosting precision.

Technical System Architecture

End-to-end data flow from user inputs to automated feature engineering, machine learning classification, and commercial acquisition recommendations:

Step 1: Input Ingestion & Reputation Index Engine
Select Director, Banner, Lead Actor, Lead Actress, and Music Director. Background engine computes reputation scores (1.0 - 10.0).
Step 2: Feature Engineering (66 Dimensions)
Vectorizes 52+ journal content themes, budget currency scaling to USD, runtime classification, and popularity tags.
Step 3: Machine Learning Inference Engine
Evaluates scaled features using Gradient Boosting Classifier to generate class probability distributions.
Step 4: Strategic Analytics & Content Acquisition
Outputs Greenlight/Acquisition badge, marketing allocation percentages, and platform distribution positioning.
